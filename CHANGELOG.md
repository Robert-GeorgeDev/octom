# Changelog

## Unreleased

- New logo: the octopus is replaced by a mark of eight rounded dashes in a wheel (violet and blue). It is used in the header, sidebar, loading screen, landing hero, social preview image, favicon and installed-app icons.
- Four new audience pages, in English and Romanian: translators, designers, photographers and small accounting practices (`/for/translators`, `/for/designers`, `/for/photographers`, `/for/accountants`). They only describe what the product does today and are in the sitemap with language twins.
- Finishing touches: search and shortcuts (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K on a computer, a magnifier button on phones) to jump to a page or find a contact by name or company; finishing a follow-up now shows a message with Undo (Undo also removes the next cadence step it had scheduled); a quiet offer to install the app on the phone (Chrome), hidden for 30 days after Not now.
- Cadences: when you add a follow-up you can pick a cadence (after a proposal, pick up a quiet contact, unpaid invoice, after a meeting). Finishing a step schedules the next one, counted from the day you finished it; a card shows the step and can stop the cadence, and a contact marked Won or Lost stops it too. Needs the database change `supabase/migrations/20261028_01_follow-up-cadences.sql`; until it is run, follow-ups load as before and cadences cannot be saved.
- New "Today" page (first item in the menu and on the phone bar): the follow-ups that are due or late, in a clear order, each with the reasons it is there (overdue by N days, proposal sent, high priority, quiet for weeks) and a Write button that opens the contact on the Templates tab. The order is explained on the page itself.
- Message templates: a new "Templates" tab on every contact with seven ready messages in Romanian and English (after a proposal, late proposal, invoice due, gone quiet, after a meeting, no answer to a call, ask for feedback). You edit the text, then copy it or open it in WhatsApp or your email app; nothing is sent automatically. The WhatsApp link now also understands Romanian numbers written as 07xx and puts the message in the chat.
- Loading states now show a soft shimmering skeleton of the page (dashboard, contacts, pipeline, contact page, activity) instead of a floating logo and the word "Loading"; screen readers still hear "Loading".
- www.octom.eu now redirects to octom.eu (same path), so search engines see one copy of every page and the sitemap host matches.
- Search and AI visibility: every public page now exists in English and Romanian (`/ro/...`) with its own title, description, canonical address and hreflang twin; legal, docs and contact pages are rendered in the right language on the server. Audience pages (`/for/...`) have problems, steps and real FAQs. Structured data (Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList), `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` for AI assistants, robots.txt that welcomes AI search crawlers, a sitemap with hreflang and stable dates, a Romanian social preview image, IndexNow for Bing and others, optional Search Console and Bing verification codes. See `docs/seo.md`.
- Database changes are now tracked: new files in `supabase/migrations/`, a small `app_migrations` table, and `/api/health` reports `"schema": true` when every required change is applied (false or null otherwise, details only in the server log). A test keeps the file list and the app's list in sync.
- CSV import is friendlier: it recognises column names from Google Contacts, LinkedIn and Excel (English and Romanian, for example Prenume, Nume, Telefon, Firmă), accepts a single name column, dates written DD.MM.YYYY, and reads job title, website, location, source and tags. The export has the same new columns.
- The add-contact form is redesigned: every field from the contact details (job title, website, location, source, tags) is on the form, plus an optional first follow-up date with Tomorrow / In 3 days / Next week shortcuts, so a new contact does not need a second visit to fill in the rest.
- In-app feedback: a Feedback button (top right on computers, top bar on phones) opens a small window to report a bug, suggest an idea or write something else. It uses the contact form's inbox; the email subject says "app" and shows the page the person was on.
- The invitation page (/join) and the first-step page (/onboarding) now use the same dark design as sign-in and sign-up.
- First minute: a new, empty workspace now shows a "Start in one minute" card on the dashboard with two choices, add the first contact or try three example contacts (with follow-ups overdue, today and later). While examples exist a banner offers to remove them with one click.
- Language in the address: the home page now exists in English (/) and Romanian (/ro), each sent as ready-made HTML in its own language (no more English flash), with hreflang links and a sitemap entry. Visitors whose browser is Romanian, or who chose Romanian before, are taken to /ro. Fixed: every public page claimed the home page as its canonical address.
- Speed: public pages no longer load the sign-in session code (only the app, join and onboarding pages do), far-below-the-fold landing sections are drawn lazily, icons are cached for a week.
- New social preview image (dark brand background with the octopus), a slightly larger octopus in the browser tab icon, and a dark theme colour for phone browsers and the installed app.
- Cleanup: removed the unused `stripe` package, two unused components, 87 unused translation texts left from older landing pages, and a few unused styles and constants. No change to what visitors see.
- Stripe, paid plans and plan limits removed. OCTOM One is free during beta: unlimited contacts and follow-ups, up to 5 people per workspace, 30 AI requests per user per day. Removed: checkout, billing portal, webhook, Plans page, usage banner, grace period and trial, plan picker on sign-up. The landing pricing section is now one free card. Run `supabase/remove-plans.sql` when deploying. Legal pages updated (Terms section 6, Privacy, Security, Subprocessors, Cookies, Billing); have them reviewed before launch.
- Early Feedback reward is now a 100% voucher for one month of Business, valid when paid plans launch.
- Log in, sign up, forgot and reset password redesigned: dark navy background with a violet-blue light beam, frosted glass card, gradient button, show/hide password. Sign-up is two columns (what you get, with a sample "Today" list) with a plan picker (Free, Pro with 7 days free, Business).
- Early Feedback banner on the landing page and the contact page: a confirmed bug or an accepted idea earns a free month of Business via a 100% voucher. The contact form repeats the rules.
- Contact form topics: bug, idea, payment or invoice, account, security, other. `/contact?topic=suggestion` opens the form with a topic chosen.
- Round social media icons (LinkedIn, Facebook, GitHub, TikTok, YouTube) in the site and legal-page footers. Links are set in `lib/social.ts`; an icon with an empty link is hidden.
- Failed payments: a paid plan now stays active for 7 days after the first failed payment, with a banner asking the owner to update the card. After that the workspace drops to Free. Run `supabase/billing-grace.sql` before deploying.
- Pro now starts with a 7-day free trial for a workspace's first subscription, no card needed. If no card is added the trial ends and nothing is charged.
- Contact form has a category (help, bug, suggestion, other) that shows in the email subject. Its rate limit is now kept in the database, so it holds across servers.
- Error log: server and browser crashes are saved to `error_events` with personal data removed, with a friendly error page and an optional email alert (max 3 per hour). Entries older than 30 days are deleted by the daily job.
- Daily summary email: sent in batches of 100 with a time budget, so a large user base no longer risks a timeout, and "today" follows each person's own time zone.
- CSV import warns about duplicates (same email or phone as an existing contact, or repeated in the file) and skips them by default.
- Dashboard: "Worth a message" lists open contacts with no follow-up and no activity for 21 days or more.
- Run `supabase/launch-readiness.sql` when deploying.
- Dashboard redesigned: soft blurred backdrop in the brand colours, rounded frosted panels, colour-coded follow-up tiles with one-tap complete, month calendar, stage funnel, today's schedule. Same data and actions as before.
- New app sidebar: collapsible (icon rail with hover menus), submenus for Contacts (by status, with counts) and Workspace, smooth animations. The choice is remembered on the device.
- Contacts: status filters, sorting, and a slide-over panel per contact (also available as a full page) with call, email, WhatsApp and website buttons, editable details (job title, company, email, phone, website, location, source), tags, activity log (notes, calls, emails, meetings), follow-ups and AI tab. Run `supabase/contacts-v2.sql` when deploying.
- Added PNG icons (apple-touch, favicon.ico, PWA manifest) generated from the octopus icon.
- Landing hero redesigned: dark opening with the animated octopus mascot, product window overlapping the hero, dark header and footer. Rest of the page unchanged.
- Redesigned landing page: neutral surfaces with a violet-blue accent, animated product window, scroll progress bar, new comparison section, rewritten copy (EN + RO). New rounded wordmark and octopus mascot in the brand gradient, new favicon.
- First public version.
- Removed Bootstrap Mode and the admin page. Plans and limits apply as before. Run `supabase/remove-bootstrap.sql` when deploying.
