This page explains how billing works for the Octom service.

This policy is part of the **Octom Terms and conditions**. If it conflicts with a mandatory legal right, the mandatory legal right applies.

## 1. Current status

Octom is currently **free to use** while it is in beta. There are no paid plans, no subscriptions and no payments, so there is nothing to bill, cancel or refund.

## 2. If paid plans are introduced

If we introduce paid plans in the future, we will:

* tell you well in advance;
* show the price, billing period, taxes and what is included before you pay;
* charge you only with your express agreement;
* publish the billing, cancellation and refund conditions here, and update the Terms and the Privacy policy, before any payment is requested.

Nothing in this page limits any legal right you have as a consumer.
